> **[A]** = assumption. Official numbers below come from Roblox Creator Hub (checked 4 Nov 2026): [Icons](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/experience-icons) · [Thumbnails](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails).

## 1. The hook each image sells
The first-3-minute fantasy (spec 01 B1): **"I'm the keeper of a growing occult menagerie. My own cryptid is already making money."**

| Asset | Sells | Shot |
|---|---|---|
| **Icon** | "A creepy creature that's *yours*" | One subject: the **Mothman Hatchling**'s face and folded ragged wings, two dark-red eyes, on a Void background with a thin Gold ritual ring behind it |
| **Thumbnail A: "Your lair"** | Collecting + income | Your walled lair at night: pad ring lit by candles, Hatchling, Hodag, Hidebehind and one Epic (Jersey Devil) on pads, Bone Altar centre, Gold `+$` floating over one pad |
| **Thumbnail B: "The hunt"** | Catching in the fog | Player's lantern beam in the Wilds fog, a half-seen Hidebehind or Beast of Bray Road, Hollowmere's lamps in the background |

Both thumbnails must be **real in-game builds**, staged with Studio's free camera (Shift+P in a solo playtest). No creature or UI that isn't in the game (Roblox's thumbnail guidance warns against misrepresenting gameplay).

## 2. Sizes and safe areas

| Asset | Official spec | Our safe area [A] |
|---|---|---|
| Icon | Square, **at least 512 × 512 px**; scales down to sizes like **150 × 150**, so preview it small | Keep the face and eyes inside the **central ~70%**. Must read at 150 px and 64 px |
| Thumbnails | **16:9, ideally 1920 × 1080**; .png/.jpg; under **3 MB** for personalisation upload; Roblox says avoid essential content at the **bottom**, where metadata like player count can cover it | Keep focal subjects in the **middle two-thirds**; nothing important in the **bottom ~15%** or outer ~5% |

- Activate **both** thumbnails: Roblox's thumbnail personalisation starts only with **2 or more** active thumbnails.
- No official icon safe-margin exists in the docs, so the 70% rule is our assumption.

## 3. Palette
Void `#0B0A0C` (dominant), Ash `#1A1719`, Soot `#2A2427`, Blood `#7A0F12` (eyes, banners), Gold `#9C7A3C` / GoldBright `#C9A55A` (ritual ring, income), Bone `#E8E0CF` (bones, highlights), Rift `#7FA34A` **rare accent only** (≤ 2% of the image, e.g. one Rift glint). Candle light `#FFB36B`. **No bright purple, saturated blue, pastel or cartoon shapes.**

## 4. Text and clutter
- **No text on the icon.** At most a 2–3 word title on thumbnails (e.g. "Cryptid Lair" [A, final name TBD]), never at the bottom.
- No prices, no "FREE", no "50% off", no fake UI, no arrows or emoji clutter. Roblox's guidance bans promo claims like "50% off" in video thumbnails; we apply the same rule to images.
- Brighter than the game feels at night: lift exposure so it reads on a dim phone, but it must still match real in-game lighting (style guide §4).
