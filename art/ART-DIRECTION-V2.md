# Art Direction v2 (Eudai, 5 Nov 2026) — supersedes the muted look in style-guide v0.8 where they conflict
Reference images: art/concepts/v2-*.jpg
- Authentic Roblox look: smooth rounded meshes and SmoothPlastic/Wood, blocky avatars. NOT Minecraft voxels, NOT dull Diablo browns.
- Spooky-cute, high contrast, saturated colours that pop: purple night sky, glowing accents, strong (budgeted) bloom.
- Coloured magic flames as a signature VFX: green, purple, blue, crimson braziers/torches. Neon is allowed for flames, portals, signs and eyes, within budgets.
- Town must show biome portals off the plaza (glowing arch + swirling vortex + floating sign + lock/level icon): Whispering Woods (green), Sunken Marsh (teal), Bone Quarry (orange).
- Shops must have iconic silhouettes (shop-as-object): potion shop in a giant cauldron, food shop in a giant jack-o'-lantern; apply the same idea to the Pedlar, Tarot Table, Bone Broker, etc.
- Case reel: rarity-coloured card frames (grey/green/blue/purple/gold), golden needle, motion blur and sparkles. Real odds per spec 10; earned tokens only, never gems/Robux for random items.
- Budgets still hold for phones: cap lights per area, particle caps per effect, a Low-quality fallback (fewer flames, no bloom).
